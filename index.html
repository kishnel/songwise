Getting Started

  1. 1 Open Songwise on your iPhone. There is no sign-up and nothing to set up beyond one permission.
  2. 2 Tap Allow Apple Music Access. Songwise reads which song is playing; it doesn't change your library.
  3. 3 Play a song in Apple Music. Songwise shows two lines about it, one about the song and one about the artist, with more on demand.
  4. 4 Put Song Summary on the Action button or the lock screen (below) to see those two lines without opening the app.
💡 Songwise works with Apple Music only. You can also search for Songwise in Spotlight and tap Song Summary.
🔘

Action Button

  1. 1 Open the Settings app and tap Action Button.
  2. 2 Swipe to Shortcut, then tap Choose a Shortcut.
  3. 3 Under Songwise, choose Song Summary.
  4. 4 Press the Action button while a song plays. A card shows the song's artwork, title, artist and the two lines. Tap Open in Songwise for more.
🔒

Lock Screen

  1. 1 Touch and hold the lock screen, tap Customize, then tap Lock Screen.
  2. 2 Tap the minus on the flashlight or camera button, then tap the empty spot.
  3. 3 Add the Shortcut control, then choose Song Summary under Songwise.
  4. 4 Press that button on the lock screen while a song plays to see its card.
🎵

Now Playing

  1. 1 The Now Playing tab follows Apple Music as songs change. It shows the summary, with play/pause, skip and a progress bar under the song.
  2. 2 Open the song, the artist, the album or its music videos for more: Apple Music's notes, Wikipedia articles, and facts such as where a band formed and who is in it.
  3. 3 Tap a track on the album page to play it in Apple Music, with the rest of the album after it. Without an Apple Music subscription, the tap plays a 30-second preview instead; tap again to stop it.
  4. 4 A song that's in your library but not in the Apple Music catalog still gets a summary, from its own tags, Wikipedia and MusicBrainz. If Songwise finds a close match by an artist with a similar name, it asks "Did you mean …?" and remembers your answer.
🕘

History

  1. 1 Every song you look up, in the app or from the card, appears in History, grouped by day, with its summary.
  2. 2 Tap an entry to open that song's page again.
  3. 3 History stays on your iPhone. Turn off Log to History in Settings to stop saving songs, or tap Clear History to delete it.
⚙️

Settings

  1. 1 Pick your instrument. "Learn it on…" then searches YouTube for lessons on it. Choose None to hide it.
  2. 2 Log to History and Clear History control what's saved.
  3. 3 How to Use Songwise shows the setup steps again.
📚

Where the Facts Come From

  1. 1 Details, notes, artwork and music videos come from the Apple Music catalog. Articles come from Wikipedia, under CC BY-SA 4.0, and each one links to its page. Facts come from MusicBrainz and Wikidata. Covers for songs Apple Music doesn't have come from the Cover Art Archive.
  2. 2 The two summary lines are written on your iPhone by Apple's on-device model, from that text only. A line that names anything the text doesn't is replaced by one taken from the text itself, but a summary can still be wrong.
  3. 3 The full list is in Settings → Sources and Licences.
