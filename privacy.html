Songwise

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Overview

Songwise is a free iPhone app that tells you about the song playing in Apple Music. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no server of ours. The app does not collect your data.

1. No Account

There is no sign-up and no login. We never create a profile for you or ask for a password.

2. Apple Music

Songwise asks for access to Apple Music so it can read which song is playing and look it up in the Apple Music catalog. It does not change your library or what plays, except when you use the play, pause and skip buttons on the Now Playing screen. You can turn access off at any time in Settings → Apps → Songwise.

3. Looking Up a Song

To tell you about a song, Songwise sends the song, album and artist names and IDs (such as the song's ISRC) to these services:

  • Apple Music catalog, for song, album and artist details, artwork and music videos
  • Wikipedia, for articles about the song and the artist
  • MusicBrainz and Wikidata, for facts such as where a band formed and who its members are
  • Cover Art Archive, for album covers of songs that aren't in the Apple Music catalog

Nothing else about you is sent. Like any website, these services see your device's IP address when it connects. Their own privacy policies apply to what they do with it.

"Learn it on…" opens a YouTube search for the song in your browser. Songwise sends nothing to YouTube itself.

4. Summaries

The two summary lines are written on your iPhone by Apple's on-device model. The text never leaves your device to make them.

5. History and Stored Data

If Log to History is on, each song you look up is saved in History on your iPhone only, with its summary and artwork. Turn the setting off to stop logging, or use Clear History in Settings to delete it all.

Wikipedia and MusicBrainz results are kept on your iPhone for 30 days so a song you look up again loads faster. Your settings, such as your instrument, are stored on your iPhone. None of this reaches us.

6. No Tracking, No Analytics, No Third-Party SDKs

Songwise does not track you across apps or websites, contains no advertising, and links no third-party analytics or crash-reporting code. The only frameworks it uses are Apple's own. The only diagnostics we may receive are Apple's aggregated, non-identifying reports through App Store Connect, which you control in your device settings.

7. Children's Privacy

Songwise is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Because no data is collected at all, the app is safe for users of all ages.

8. Changes to This Policy

If we ever change this policy, the updated version will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date. We will never introduce data collection without clearly communicating it first.
